## 1. Problem Statement & Official Requirements

### 1.1 Background & Physics of Infrasound
Infrasound refers to acoustic pressure waves propagating through the atmosphere at frequencies below the human auditory threshold (**< 20 Hz**). Due to low atmospheric attenuation at these low frequencies, infrasound waves travel thousands of kilometers. They are generated by high-energy geophysical and anthropogenic events, including:
* **Natural Sources:** Volcanic eruptions, bolide/meteor airbursts, severe severe weather fronts, earthquakes, and avalanches.
* **Human-Made Sources:** Distant industrial explosions, rocket launches, supersonic aircraft, and nuclear tests.

Precision detection requires a specialized **microbarometer** capable of resolving micro-Pascal ($\mu	ext{Pa}$) to Pascal ($	ext{Pa}$) fluctuations on top of the massive ambient atmospheric static pressure ($pprox 101,325	ext{ Pa}$).

---

### 1.2 Official Hardware Architecture Scope
The problem statement requires addressing the complete hardware architecture:
1. **(a) Pressure Sensing Mechanism:** High-compliance diaphragm or piezoresistive/capacitive MEMS sensor.
2. **(b) Mechanical Transducer Design:** Rigid, low-deformation chamber isolating acoustic from thermal expansion.
3. **(c) Differential Pressure Measurement Technique:** Measuring pressure relative to an internal reference chamber rather than absolute pressure.
4. **(d) Low-Noise Analog Front-End Electronics:** Ultra-low input noise pre-amplification and active bandpass conditioning.
5. **(e) Temperature Compensation:** Real-time thermal drift correction ($P_{	ext{comp}} = P_{	ext{raw}} - f(T)$).
6. **(f) Long-Period Pressure Equalization System:** Calibrated acoustic capillary leak establishing high-pass cutoff ($f_c \le 0.01	ext{ Hz}$).
7. **(g) Environmental Enclosure:** Weatherproof, EMI-shielded enclosure.
8. **(h) Wind-Noise Reduction Interface:** Porous hose array or multi-port spatial averaging manifold to suppress turbulent wind noise.
9. **(i) Calibration Methodology:** Traceable physical and dynamic calibration across the entire passband.

---

## 2. Complete Engineering Solution Architecture

### 2.1 Mechanical Engineering Solution
* **Differential Reference Chamber:** A rigid $100	ext{ mL}$ sealed aluminum enclosure providing a stable baseline volume ($V_0$).
* **Capillary Equalization Leak:** An acoustic capillary tube providing acoustic resistance $R_a$ coupled with chamber acoustic compliance $C_a$.
  $$	au = R_a C_a \ge 16.0	ext{ s} \implies f_{	ext{low}} = rac{1}{2\pi 	au} \le 0.01	ext{ Hz}$$
  This allows slow atmospheric weather drifts ($<0.001	ext{ Hz}$) to equalize between sides without saturating the diaphragm, while capturing dynamic infrasonic waves ($0.01–20	ext{ Hz}$).
* **Wind-Noise Reduction Interface:** Spatial averaging multi-port array with porous foam plugs. Turbulent wind eddies are spatially incoherent and cancel out across the manifold, while acoustic infrasound waves (long wavelength $\lambda > 17	ext{ m}$) arrive coherently with zero attenuation.
* **Environmental Packaging:** IP67 weatherproof housing with silicone shock-absorber isolation mounts preventing desk/ground vibrations from coupling into the diaphragm.

---

### 2.2 Electrical & Analog Electronics Solution
* **Transducer Stage:** High-sensitivity differential capacitive/piezoresistive sensing diaphragm.
* **Preamplifier Stage:** Ultra-low noise instrumentation amplifier (**INA333 / INA128**) with input voltage noise density $<50	ext{ nV}/\sqrt{	ext{Hz}}$ and high Common-Mode Rejection Ratio (CMRR $>100	ext{ dB}$).
* **Analog Filtering:** 4th-order active Sallen-Key bandpass filter ($0.01	ext{ Hz} - 20	ext{ Hz}$) preventing high-frequency alias folding and DC rail saturation.
* **Output Driver Stage:** Dual-rail op-amp driver powered by $\pm 15	ext{ V}$ rails, delivering standard $\pm 12	ext{ V}$ differential analog output ($24	ext{ V}$ peak-to-peak) with $20	ext{ mV/Pa}$ sensitivity.
* **24-Bit Digitization:** High-precision $\Delta\Sigma$ 24-bit ADC (**ADS1220 / ADS1262**) providing $>115	ext{ dB}$ dynamic range, resolving micro-Pascal variations ($<0.0001	ext{ Pa}$) well exceeding the $<0.05	ext{ Pa}$ resolution requirement.

---

### 2.3 Embedded DAQ Firmware Solution (ESP32)
* **Sampling Engine:** Hardware timer-driven interrupt triggering ADC conversion at exactly $100.0	ext{ Hz}$ ($10	ext{ ms}$ period) with sub-microsecond jitter.
* **Packet Protocol:** Timestamped telemetry frames streamed over USB UART at 115200 baud:
  `[timestamp_ms, raw_voltage_mv, temperature_c, wind_level]`

---

* **Architecture:** Python Streamlit real-time dashboard + SQLite time-series engine.

## 3. Calibration Methodology Deep-Dive

The project implements two complementary calibration methods:

### 3.1 Dynamic Comparison Method ($0.5 - 20	ext{ Hz}$)
* **Apparatus:** Airtight acoustic test chamber housing the Device Under Test (DUT) and a calibrated reference manometer. A low-frequency subwoofer driven by a function generator generates pure sinusoidal pressure oscillations at $0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 5.0, 10.0, 15.0, 20.0	ext{ Hz}$.
* **Mathematical Extraction:**
  * Linear regression between $\Delta P_{	ext{ref}}$ (Pa) and $V_{	ext{DUT}}$ (mV):
    $$V_{	ext{DUT}} = S \cdot \Delta P_{	ext{ref}} + V_{	ext{offset}}$$
  * Confirms $S = 20.0	ext{ mV/Pa}$ with linearity $R^2 \ge 0.99$.
  * Frequency response Bode plot verifies passband flatness within $\pm 1	ext{ dB}$.

### 3.2 Quasi-Static Syringe Method ($< 0.1	ext{ Hz}$)
* **Apparatus:** A precision micrometer syringe injects a step volume displacement $\Delta V$ into the reference chamber of volume $V_0$.
* **Mathematical Extraction:**
  * Boyle's Law step pressure: $\Delta P_0 = - P_0 rac{\Delta V}{V_0}$.
  * Exponential decay fitting of the capillary equalization leak: $V(t) = V_0 e^{-t/	au}$.
  * Low-frequency cutoff extraction: $f_{	ext{low}} = rac{1}{2\pi 	au}$.
  * Proves $f_{	ext{low}} \le 0.01	ext{ Hz}$ ($\le 10	ext{ mHz}$) when $	au \ge 16	ext{ s}$.
